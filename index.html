Lira: Rune Forest – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Lira: Rune Forest ("the game", "we") is developed by Nerly Biton. This policy explains what information the game and the services it uses may collect, why, and the choices you have. It covers the game on Android and iOS.

Summary

  • You do not need an account to play. We never ask for your name, email or phone number in the game.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games or Apple Game Center, it can also be saved to your account there.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob and offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play or the Apple App Store.
  • We use anonymous analytics and crash reports to make the game better.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress is saved locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. This includes levels, stars, coins, lives, boosters, outfits and settings. Uninstalling the game may delete it, unless your device or game account restores it from a backup.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads, including optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch for in-game rewards. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID (such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA)
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device information such as model, operating system, language and screen size
  • how you interact with ads

This information is used to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. Depending on your choices and where you live, ads may be personalised.

Your choices: In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, through Google's consent message. You can change this choice at any time from the game's settings. On iOS, you are asked for permission before your device's advertising identifier is used for tracking. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings: on Android, Settings → Google → Ads; on iOS, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

The game offers optional purchases, such as coins, lives, boosters, outfits and removing ads. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store, and we never receive or store your payment card details. We receive a confirmation of the purchase, including an order ID, product and time, so we can deliver your items and restore them. We may use a purchase-management service, such as RevenueCat, to verify purchases. That service receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts.

The game does not sell random, chance-based items such as loot boxes. You always know what you are buying.

Game accounts and cloud save (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games or Apple Game Center, we receive your player ID and display name from that service, so we can save your progress to the cloud and show achievements or leaderboards. You can play without signing in.

Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase services, including Analytics and Crashlytics, to understand how the game is played, such as which levels are too hard and where players stop. They also tell us about crashes so we can fix them. These services collect an app instance ID, device and app information, events in the game and crash logs. They do not collect your name or contact details.

Other third-party services

The game may load fonts from Google Fonts, which can log standard technical information such as your IP address.

The third parties named in this policy process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google (AdMob, Firebase, Play Games, Google Play)
  • Apple
  • RevenueCat

Legal basis (EEA/UK users)

We process data based on:

  • your consent, for personalised ads;
  • our legitimate interest in running, improving and securing the game, for analytics and crash reports;
  • the need to perform our agreement with you, for delivering purchases.

Data retention

Progress stored on your device stays there until you delete the game or its data. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. Purchase records are kept as long as needed to restore purchases and meet legal obligations. Third-party services keep data as described in their own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the EU, the UK and California, you may have the right to:

  • access, correct or delete your personal information;
  • object to or restrict its processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time.

To use these rights, contact us at the email below. Because the game has no accounts, we may need your app user ID, which is shown in the game's settings, to find your data.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use the security measures offered by the services listed above, and data sent over the internet is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the game.
